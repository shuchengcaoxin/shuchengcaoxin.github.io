01 Methods

The transferable core

The methods come first—they are what transfers. Genetics, medicine, and molecular science are where I have tested them most deeply so far.

Inference

Causal inference from observational data

I use natural experiments, instrumental-variable designs, replication, and sensitivity analysis to distinguish signals that may support decisions from associations that only look persuasive.

Scale

Statistical modelling at scale

I work with high-dimensional data where harmonisation, multiple testing, reproducible batch computation, and explicit diagnostics matter as much as fitting the model itself.

Evaluation

Machine learning under distribution shift

I test whether models retain performance outside familiar benchmarks, with an emphasis on out-of-distribution evaluation, leakage, robustness, and reproducible comparisons.

Systems

Auditable AI and evidence pipelines

I build research systems that preserve source provenance, separate retrieved evidence from generated interpretation, and expose unsupported claims rather than hiding them.

A working ruleIf I cannot reconstruct the inputs, transformations, assumptions, validation checks, and failure modes behind a result, I do not regard it as finished. That rule applies to statistical analyses, machine-learning evaluations, and AI-assisted systems.

02 Applications

Where I apply it

The domains change, but the quantitative questions repeat: identification, robustness, generalisation, controlled simulation, and evidence quality.

01 Statistical inference
McGill

Causal inference with multi-stage validation

I designed an automated workflow over GWAS and pQTL data from more than 790,000 participants. Candidate signals must reproduce across independent measurement platforms and survive colocalization and sensitivity checks. Four remained after the full sequence; metabolic liver disease is the current application, and the manuscript is in revision.

02 Applied AI
CABS 2026

Evidence-grounded LLM system

I led the engineering of CausalSentinel / OpenCausal, which joins 101,543 published estimates with nine public data sources. Its reports are rendered from a structured ledger, and validation compares model-written interpretation with retrieved evidence; contradictions are marked as failed rather than quietly published.

03 Model evaluation
IEEE TPAMI

Out-of-distribution evaluation for 3D vision

On AbdomenCT-1K, I helped build and evaluate a public benchmark containing more than 1,000 scans from 12 medical centres. Its central question was generalisation: how much do leading segmentation systems degrade when tested on institutions outside their training distribution?

04 Simulation
KAUST

Controlled simulation for mechanism discovery

I used controlled molecular-dynamics experiments to isolate the effects of hydrophobicity and aromaticity on self-assembly, then translated the inferred mechanism into a ranking of new peptide designs. The simulation functioned as a controlled computational experiment.

Core toolkit: causal inference and statistical modelling · robustness and model validation · machine-learning evaluation · simulation · data integration and provenance · Python, R, PyTorch, Linux/HPC
