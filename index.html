01 Now

What is on my desk

I currently split my time between a proteome-wide causal analysis for my thesis and OpenCausal, the research tool I led during my CABS internship.

Thesis / 2024–present

Proteins and metabolic disease

I am screening circulating proteins for causal effects on MASLD and obesity-related disease. The analysis spans data from more than 790,000 people. A signal stays only if it reproduces across two independent proteomic platforms and survives colocalization and sensitivity checks.

CABS / 2026

CausalSentinel / OpenCausal

During my CABS internship, I led the engineering of an agent that joins published MR estimates with nine public biomedical databases. It now serves 991 protein dossiers, with the source evidence kept alongside each result.

Repository Dossiers

02 Selected work

Selected projects

These four projects trace how my work moved from molecular simulation and medical imaging into causal genetics and research software.

01 PhD research
McGill

Proteome-wide causal screen

I built a multi-stage MR and colocalization workflow for metabolic liver disease, then required promising proteins to reproduce on independent measurement platforms. Four candidates remained after those checks; the manuscript is in revision.

02 Research tool
CABS 2026

CausalSentinel / OpenCausal

The public system combines 101,543 published MR estimates with evidence from genetics and drug-development databases. The output is rendered from a structured ledger; if model-written prose contradicts the retrieved evidence, the card is marked as failed rather than quietly published.

03 Medical imaging
IEEE TPAMI

AbdomenCT-1K

I helped build and evaluate a public abdominal CT benchmark with more than 1,000 scans from 12 medical centres. The study measured how much leading segmentation systems lost when they met data from hospitals outside their training distribution.

04 Molecular simulation
KAUST

Ultrashort peptide self-assembly

For my MSc, I used controlled molecular-dynamics simulations to separate the effects of hydrophobicity and aromaticity on peptide assembly, then used the resulting mechanism to rank new designs.

Methods used across these projects: Mendelian randomization, colocalization, GWAS and pQTL analysis, survival and mixed-effects models, PyTorch, molecular dynamics, R, Python, and Linux/HPC.
